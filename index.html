About

Researcher, trainer and teacher serving Bangladesh's civil service since 1995.

I work in research, education and adult-learning training. My expertise spans management, leadership, organizational behavior, industrial relations, HRM, and quantitative and qualitative research using SPSS, STATA, SEM, SmartPLS and Excel. At BPATC I conduct training sessions, manage courses, lead research and develop curricula. I have also supervised nearly 100 policy papers written by Joint Secretaries and Deputy Secretaries.

FAQ

Is the help free?

Basic guidance and the tools on this site are free. Formal consultancy, training or supervision is arranged by agreement. Please message first.

Do you guide students outside BPATC?

Yes, subject to time. Send your topic, level and deadline through the form below.

Which statistical tools do you work with?

SPSS, STATA, SEM, SmartPLS and Excel, for both quantitative and qualitative research.

Can you run training for my organization?

Yes. Training design, TOT, leadership, HRM, public sector management and training needs assessment.

How fast will I get a reply?

Usually within a few working days. For urgent matters, use WhatsApp.
